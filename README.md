## Overview
**SkyVolt Logistics** builds co-located urban micro-hubs in Nairobi that solve two fast-growing infrastructure bottlenecks simultaneously:
1. **Quick-Commerce Road Gridlock:** High-volume restaurants and cloud kitchens on Glovo and Bolt Food lose repeat orders to 45–60 minute peak-hour traffic delays. SkyVolt deploys short-range autonomous payload drones to deliver priority food orders within a 4 km radius in **under 10 minutes**.
2. **E-Bike Charging Deficits:** Electric boda-boda riders (Roam, Spiro, Ampersand) lose hours of daily income waiting at scarce charging points. Our street-level fast-charging bays generate **Day-1 M-PESA cash flow** while sharing grid and solar-hybrid power infrastructure with our rooftop drone fleet.

Two Bottlenecks Slowing Nairobi's Delivery Boom

Quick-commerce and electric two-wheelers are growing fast, yet urban infrastructure lags behind.

Quick-Commerce Road Gridlock

Restaurants on Glovo and Bolt Food lose repeat orders daily to 45–60 minute peak-hour traffic delays, cold meals, and steep courier fees.

E-Bike Charging Deficits

Electric boda-boda riders (Roam, Spiro, Ampersand) lose 1–2 hours of daily income waiting in queues at scarce charging stations.

The SkyVolt Co-Located Micro-Hub

One urban lease powering two high-margin revenue streams.

1. Ground Bay: Cash Flow

Commercial fast-charging bays generating daily M-PESA revenue from Day 1.

  • Open to all third-party e-boda riders
  • Automated M-PESA Daraja billing
  • Solar-hybrid grid backup

2. Rooftop: Aerial Speed

Short-range payload drones bypass road gridlock for priority food orders.

  • Under 10-minute delivery radius
  • Thermal food-grade payload boxes
  • Geofenced, KCAA-compliant routes

3. Shared Economics

Shared power and security lower CapEx and fixed costs per transaction.

  • Charging covers fixed hub rent & OpEx
  • High-margin B2B delivery upside
  • Replicable across Nairobi corridors
